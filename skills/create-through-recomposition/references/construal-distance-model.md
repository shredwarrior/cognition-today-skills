## Construal levels

`[S]` The same object can be represented at different levels of construal. Concrete and abstract construals expose different properties and make different conceptual associations available.

| Level | Processing bias `[S]` | Creative use `[A]` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Concrete, local, detailed, attribute-sensitive | Add specificity, texture, implementation detail, distinctive signals, or discriminating evidence |
| Intermediate | Category, function, relationship, mechanism | Reorganize modules, roles, causal structure, genre, or system behaviour |
| High | Abstract, global, essence-oriented, broad | Access distant categories, alternate purposes, analogies, themes, or problem frames |

`[A]` High construal is not better. It is differently useful. Treating abstraction as sophistication produces vague, unbuildable output.

Worked example of one object at three levels:

| Level | "A bicycle" |
| --- | --- |
| Low | Steel frame, chain, two wheels, handlebars, a saddle at a fixed height |
| Intermediate | A human-powered mechanism that converts leg rotation into forward motion while the rider stays balanced |
| High | A way to extend a body's range without external fuel |

`[D]` Each level yields different neighbours. At low construal the neighbours are other objects with chains and wheels. At high construal the neighbours include shoes, elevators, and remote work. The distant neighbours are only reachable from the abstract description.

## Local and global processing

`[S]` Construal level is associated with a bias toward local, detail-oriented processing at low levels and global, essence-oriented processing at high levels.

`[A]` Diagnose which mode the current work is stuck in:

| Symptom | Current mode | Move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Endless refinement of details that do not change the whole | Locked local | Raise construal, restate the purpose |
| Grand framing that cannot be executed | Locked global | Lower construal, name the first concrete artifact |
| Detail-perfect but characterless | Locked local | Raise once to find a governing idea, then return |
| Conceptually striking but incoherent | Locked global | Lower to restore causal relations and invariants |

## Construal traversal as neighbour access

`[D]` The operational value of construal theory for an agent is that abstraction level determines the reachable candidate set. When generation keeps returning obvious options, the level is the constraint, not the effort.

`[A]` When the current neighbour set is exhausted, move at least one level before generating again. Do not generate harder at the same level.

`[A]` Traversal is a round trip. Raise to find the possibility, lower to make it real. A skill that only raises produces manifestos; one that only lowers produces polish.

## Psychological distance

`[S]` Psychological distance and construal level are linked: greater distance is associated with more abstract construal. Distance can be varied along informational, personal, temporal, and spatial dimensions.

| Distance | Prompt transformation `[A]` | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Informational | Rename the object at a broader or narrower categorical level | "Onboarding flow" becomes "the first promise a product makes" |
| Personal | Solve for another person, audience, stakeholder, or a third-person version of the self | "How would a hostile reviewer describe this?" |
| Temporal | Consider how the problem appears in the distant future, the distant past, or after incubation | "How would this have been solved before the internet?" |
| Spatial | Relocate the scenario to another environment, market, culture, scale, or physical context | "What is the version of this that works in a village of 300 people?" |

`[A]` Psychological distance is one operator family. Do not force all four forms into every task. Pick the dimension along which the current representation is most rigidly fixed.

`[A]` Do not impersonate lived experience when using personal distance. Adopting a stakeholder's viewpoint to surface neglected properties is legitimate; asserting what a real group feels is not.

`[?]` The source's treatment of the empirical evidence linking distance and creative performance is not transcribed in the master specification. Do not make effect-size or causal claims from this file.

## The analytical and holistic caveat

`[S]` The source cautions that the mapping between construal level, distance, and creative benefit is not uniform. Analytical and holistic processing styles interact with construal, and abstraction does not reliably improve every kind of creative task.

`[A]` Treat construal shifting as a tool with a diagnosis, not a general performance enhancer. Do not tell a user that thinking more abstractly will make them more creative.

`[A]` Preserve this caveat in any public description of the skill. Removing it would convert a qualified source into an overclaim.

## Returning to low construal

`[A]` Execution happens at low construal. Before delivering, verify that the selected candidate has:

- Concrete components a reader can act on
- Specific rather than categorical language
- Named constraints it satisfies
- A first step that could be taken today
- Restored coherence in its causal or functional relations

`[A]` If a candidate cannot survive this check, it is a direction, not a deliverable. Say so explicitly rather than dressing it up.

## Worked traversals

**Stuck design task.** "Make the dashboard less cluttered." Low construal generates spacing and font tweaks. Raise to intermediate: what job does the dashboard do, and for whom, in what order? That exposes a sequencing question. Raise to high: is a dashboard the right form for a question that gets asked once a week? That exposes an alternative — a weekly digest. Lower again to build whichever survived.

**Stuck naming task.** "Names for a habit-tracking app." Low construal produces habit-and-tracker compounds. Raise: the product is about the gap between intention and action. That neighbour set includes distance, drift, and return. Lower again to concrete candidates drawn from that set.

**Stuck technical task.** "The cache keeps going stale." Low construal produces another invalidation patch. Raise to intermediate: what guarantees does the consumer actually need? Possibly staleness bounds rather than freshness. Raise to high: is caching the mechanism, or is the real requirement predictable latency? Lower to a falsifiable test of the surviving hypothesis, then leave creative mode and debug.
